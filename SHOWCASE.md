## Interview Talking Points

### When Asked: "Tell me about a project you're proud of"

**Answer:**

"I built a competitive intelligence system that automates something I was spending 10+ hours a week on manually.

As a product marketer, tracking competitors is critical but tedious. I was manually checking pricing pages, feature pages, blogs - taking screenshots, comparing changes, trying to spot patterns.

So I built a system to do it automatically:
- It monitors competitor websites 24/7
- Detects when anything changes - pricing, features, messaging
- Uses AI to analyze what the change means strategically
- Generates weekly intelligence briefs automatically

The impact was immediate. We caught a competitor price increase two days before our sales team would've found out from a lost deal. That insight alone probably saved us 6 figures.

But more importantly, it freed up my time to do strategic work instead of manual tracking. And it gave us historical data we never had before.

The technical build was interesting too - I used Python for scraping, SQLite for tracking changes over time, and Claude's API for the strategic analysis.

What I'm most proud of: It demonstrated that product marketers can be builders, not just consumers of tools."

**Why this works:**
- Shows initiative
- Demonstrates technical capability
- Quantifies business impact
- Tells a story
- Positions you as a builder

---

### When Asked: "How do you stay on top of competitors?"

**Answer:**

"I built an automated system for it.

Most marketers track competitors manually - checking websites, reading newsletters, asking sales. That's reactive.

I wanted proactive intelligence, so I built a system that:
1. Monitors competitor pages daily
2. Alerts me when changes happen
3. Uses AI to analyze strategic implications

For example, last month it detected that three competitors all removed a specific feature within two weeks. That signaled the feature wasn't valuable to customers - so we deprioritized it on our roadmap.

The system has caught pricing changes, messaging shifts, and partnership announcements before they hit our sales team's radar.

It's open source if you want to see how it works."

**Why this works:**
- Concrete example
- Shows strategic thinking
- Demonstrates proactivity
- Offers to share knowledge
