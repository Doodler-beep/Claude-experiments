Competitive Intelligence Command Center — From Insight to System

Started on a weekend, refined during the week, showcased the next weekend. Built by a product marketer exploring how PMMs can operationalise strategy with AI. This is the thinking behind what I built, what broke, and what I learned.

Problem → Product Thinking → System Design → What Broke → Working Product → What's Next

Problem

3
Problem Statement

Competitive intelligence is broken

  • Manual — hours spent checking competitor sites by hand
  • Fragmented — data lives in screenshots, docs, and memory
  • Reactive — you find out after the market has already moved

PMMs spend weeks reacting instead of positioning.

"I wanted a system that thinks before the market shifts, not after."

Personal Trigger
  • Ryanair case — MBA case study on strategy vs. plans
  • Late-night LinkedIn scroll — yet another CI tools post
  • Frustration with tools that show data, not meaning

Product Thinking

3
JTBD / Use Cases
  • As a PMM, I want to know when competitors change pricing so I can update battle cards within 24h
  • As a founder, I want to spot market gaps before competitors fill them
  • As a strategy lead, I want weekly intelligence briefs without manual research
Non-Goals
  • Not enterprise-scale
  • Not multi-tenant (yet)
  • Not pixel-perfect UI

Scope decisions made to ship in a weekend.

Why start with a weekend?

Constraints force clarity. The core was built in a weekend to prove the idea works. Then refined during the week and showcased the next weekend. Shipping fast, then iterating.

"Designed for clarity, not cleverness."
